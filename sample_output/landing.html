Looking for chicken biryani near you in Richardson? Our biryani is cooked in the Chettinad style, with whole spices roasted in-house and seeraga samba or basmati rice layered over tender halal chicken. It is the dish most guests order on their first visit.

Our kitchen follows the cooking of the Chettinad region of Tamil Nadu, known for freshly roasted and ground spices: black pepper, fennel, star anise, dried red chilies, kalpasi (stone flower) and curry leaves. We roast our masalas in small batches every morning, so the flavor is deep and aromatic rather than just hot. If you prefer milder food, tell your server and the kitchen will adjust the spice level for you.

The menu centers on three dishes our regulars come back for. Chicken biryani [price] is layered with seeded basmati rice, slow-cooked chicken, mint and fried onions, and it comes with raita and salna. Chettinad chicken [price] is a dry-roasted pepper curry that pairs well with parotta or steamed rice. Dosa [price] is a thin, crisp rice-and-lentil crepe served with sambar and fresh chutneys, with masala, ghee roast and onion versions available.

All of our meat is halal. On weekdays we run a lunch buffet with rotating curries, biryani, dosa made to order and dessert, a quick option if you are coming from class or the office. We stay open until 11 PM, so dinner after a late lab or a game is easy.

You will find us in Richardson, TX 75080, a short drive from the UT Dallas campus. Dine in, pick up, or call ahead for large group orders. Hours: open daily from 11 AM to 11 PM.
